    Preparation guidance

      Photo-based identification is an estimate. Appearance alone cannot reliably confirm alloy or grade; verify with a magnet, markings, density/conductivity checks, or a yard's testing procedure as appropriate.

      03 / Processing options

      Choose your approach
      After identification, choose a preparation route. The right option depends on the metal, the item, the yard's rules, your tools, and safety.
      Identify the item first to see tailored preparation options. Until then, do not cut, burn, grind, or open unknown items.
      Processing advice is a general guide, not a guarantee that a yard will pay more. Call the receiving yard first: some prefer items intact, and removing attachments can reduce value or create hazards.

      05 / Safety before you strip it

      Always verify
      Never cut open sealed cylinders, capacitors, batteries, refrigerant equipment, or unknown containers. Stop if the item could be hazardous.
